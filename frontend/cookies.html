Cookie Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026 · Last reviewed: 4 November 2026

Authentication cookie

When you sign in, the backend sets an agroscan_session cookie configured as Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=None, with an intended maximum age of seven days. It authenticates account requests and cannot be read by JavaScript.

Browser storage

The website uses session storage for signed-in interface data. Some features may use browser storage for preferences or cart information. Clearing site data or using private browsing may remove these values and require you to sign in again.

PWA cache

The service worker may cache selected public files for offline access. Current rules are designed to bypass API responses and private developer pages. Signing out does not necessarily clear public static assets cached by the browser.

Managing storage

You can clear cookies and site data through browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent sign-in. Browser controls differ by device and browser.

Changes

This policy may be updated when authentication, browser storage or offline functionality changes.
