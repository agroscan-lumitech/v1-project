AgroScan AI: Frequently Asked Questions

Short, direct answers about the platform, how its tools work and how to verify agricultural decisions.

What is AgroScan AI?

AgroScan AI is an independent agricultural technology web platform with soil-data assessment tools, AI-assisted farming guidance, agricultural learning resources and farm-management features.

How does the AgroScan soil recommendation work?

The workflow accepts user-entered soil pH and moisture measurements, with optional temperature, location and desired crop information. It combines rule-based checks with an AI-generated response. Results depend on the quality of the information entered.

Is an AgroScan recommendation the same as a laboratory soil test?

No. AgroScan uses entered measurements and software-generated guidance; it does not issue a certified laboratory report. For a complete soil assessment, use an accredited or government-recognised soil testing laboratory and consult the official Soil Health Card portal.

Can AgroScan guarantee higher crop yields or profits?

No. Crop outcomes depend on many local factors, including weather, seed variety, soil condition, irrigation, pests, disease, farm practices and market prices. AgroScan does not guarantee yield, income or profit.

Should I follow an AI recommendation without checking it?

No. Treat AI output as decision-support information. Cross-check fertiliser, pesticide, irrigation and crop decisions with current local advisories, a soil laboratory report and a qualified agricultural professional or local extension service.

Where can farmers in India verify agricultural information?

Useful official references include the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR), the Government of India's Soil Health Card portal and the India Meteorological Department's agricultural meteorology services. State and district advisories may provide more locally relevant guidance.

Does AgroScan represent a government department?

No. AgroScan AI is an independent project and is not an official government portal or an affiliated service of ICAR, the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, IMD or the Soil Health Card programme.

Which parts of AgroScan are public?

The public site includes platform information and agricultural learning resources. Account-based tools such as saved farm records and personal analytics are intended for signed-in users.
