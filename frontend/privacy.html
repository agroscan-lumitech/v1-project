Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026 · Last reviewed: 4 November 2026

Information collected

AgroScan may process account details (name, username, email and phone), farm and land details, soil measurements, crop selections, AI chat prompts and replies, feedback, order details and profile images that you choose to submit.

Use of information

Information is used to authenticate accounts, provide farm tools and recommendations, save requested farm history, support orders where enabled, respond to feedback, prevent abuse and maintain the service.

Storage and processors

The current backend is configured to persist application records in a private GitHub-hosted data repository. Hosting and infrastructure providers process data to operate the service. Private account data is not intended to be public website content.

AI features

When you use AI chat or AI-assisted recommendations, prompts, measurements and relevant context may be sent to the configured AI provider, including Perplexity integrations present in the codebase. Avoid entering sensitive personal information. Provider processing is subject to that provider's own terms and privacy policy.

Cookies and local storage

Sign-in uses a Secure, HttpOnly session cookie with an intended seven-day lifetime. The interface also uses browser session storage. The PWA service worker is designed to cache approved public assets and bypass API responses and private developer pages. See the Cookie Policy.

Retention and deletion

Account and farm records are intended to be retained while needed to provide the service. An account-deletion flow exists for primary user records. Copies in pending synchronization journals, provider backups or security logs may persist until their applicable processing or retention cycle completes. Use in-app feedback or contact maintainers through the official project repository for access, correction or deletion assistance.

Security and choices

Authentication, access controls and private storage are used to protect account data, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. You choose what farm details and prompts to submit and should keep credentials confidential. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location and the law that applies.

Changes

This policy may change as the application and its providers change. The effective date above will be updated when a material revision is published.
